PART 1b - DARK MODE (LIGHT / DARK THEME)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------

  There is a theme button in the top navigation bar:

      🌙 Dark    -> click it to switch to dark mode
      ☀️ Light   -> click it again to switch back to light mode

  - Your choice is saved in the browser (localStorage), so after a refresh
    the page stays in the theme you picked.
  - It works on every page: login, register, passenger, driver, admin,
    tables, forms, buttons and the map pages.
  - The map also turns dark (the streets are color-inverted), but the pins
    (A, B, taxi), the green route line and the driver locations keep their
    colors, so everything stays readable.
  - Nothing is saved on the server and no database column is used for this.

PART 1c - WHAT YOU WILL SEE (ANIMATIONS AND NOTIFICATIONS)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------

  The app now has small animations so the demo looks polished:

  1. WHEN YOU REQUEST A TAXI
     A card appears with a bouncing taxi and "Finding a driver..."
     then it changes to "Driver Found" with the driver name,
     and then it opens the ride page automatically.

  2. RIDE STATUS TRACKER
     On the ride page there are 4 steps:

        📝 Requested  ->  🚕 Driver Found  ->  🟢 Ride Started  ->  🏁 Completed

     Finished steps get a green tick, the current step is highlighted.

  3. TOAST NOTIFICATIONS
     Small messages slide in at the bottom right corner:
     "Ride requested successfully", "Driver found", "Ride completed",
     etc. They disappear by themselves after a few seconds
     (you can also close them with the X).

     When a driver is assigned, the taxi icon moves smoothly from the
     driver position to your pickup point. This is only a visual effect
     for the demo - it is NOT real GPS.

  5. WHEN THE RIDE IS COMPLETED
     A small "🎉 Ride Completed!" card appears with a soft animation.

  All of these work in BOTH light mode and dark mode.

PART 4 - HOW TO USE: PASSENGER (the main demo)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 1: LOGIN
  - Go to http://localhost:3000
  - Email:    passenger@taxi.local
  - Password: passenger123
  - Click "Login"
  - You land on the Passenger Dashboard.
  - Optional: click the "🌙 Dark" button in the top bar to show dark mode.

STEP 2: WHAT YOU SEE ON THE DASHBOARD
  - "Current rides" (rides that are not finished yet)
  - "Completed rides"
  - A table of the recent rides with a colored status badge
  - A green "Request Taxi" button in the corner

STEP 3: REQUEST A TAXI (with the map)
  - Click "Request Taxi"
  - The Tehran map opens. The small taxi icons are the available drivers.
  - Click "Select Origin" then click a point on the map  -> green pin "A"
  - Click a second point -> red pin "B" (this happens automatically)
  - You can drag a pin to move it, or press "Reset" to start over
  - Click "Calculate Route"
      -> a green line appears (the shortest road route)
      -> Distance, Estimated time and Estimated price are shown
         Example: 12.1 km / 24 min / 171,000 Toman
  - Click "Request Taxi"
  - DONE: the system finds the NEAREST driver automatically.
    You are taken to the ride details page and you see:
      - your ride number (for example #4)
      - the status ACCEPTED
      - the driver name and where the driver is (for example "near Vanak")
      - the map with A (you), B (destination) and the taxi (your driver)

STEP 4: WATCH YOUR RIDE
  - The status badge updates by itself every few seconds
    (REQUESTED -> ACCEPTED -> STARTED -> COMPLETED)
  - "My Rides" shows the full list with distance and price columns

STEP 5: CANCEL A RIDE (only before it starts)
  - Go to "My Rides"
  - Next to a REQUESTED or ACCEPTED ride, click "Cancel"
  - Confirm the popup -> the status becomes CANCELLED
  - A STARTED or COMPLETED ride cannot be cancelled (the button disappears)

NOTE - REQUEST WITHOUT THE MAP:
  You can also just type an address in the Origin and Destination boxes and
  press "Request Taxi". Then there is no automatic driver, the ride stays
  REQUESTED and any driver can accept it manually.

PART 8 - WHAT THE SYSTEM DOES BEHIND THE SCENES
----------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE MAP
  Leaflet draws the map, OpenStreetMap provides the street pictures.
  Both are free and need no API key or Google Maps.

THE ROUTE (Dijkstra shortest path - the university topic)
  1. Your two map points are snapped to the nearest points of a small
     simplified Tehran road network (13 places, 20 roads, for example
     Azadi - Sadeghieh - Vanak - Tajrish - Airport).
  2. Dijkstra's algorithm finds the shortest path through that network.
  3. The result is drawn as the green line, together with the distance.
  4. If you pick a point far outside Tehran, the system shows a friendly
     message and falls back to the straight-line distance instead of
     crashing.

THE PRICE (very simple, no surge/traffic/discounts)
  price    = 50,000 + (distance in km x 10,000)   Toman, rounded
  Example: 12.1 km -> 50,000 + 121,000 = 171,000 Toman

THE TIME
  time     = distance / 30 km/h   (30 km/h is the average city speed)
  Example: 12.1 km -> about 24 minutes

THE DRIVER ASSIGNMENT (simple nearest-driver)
  1. All free drivers are collected (active, has a location, no current ride)
  2. The straight-line distance from your pickup point to each driver
     is calculated
  3. The nearest driver gets the ride and the ride starts as ACCEPTED

  These numbers (base fare, price per km, speed) live in ONE file:
  src\config.js - change them there and the whole system uses the new values.

PART 9 - THE RIDE STATUS RULES
----------------------------------------------------------------------------

  REQUESTED  -> ACCEPTED  (driver accepts, or automatic assignment)
  REQUESTED  -> CANCELLED (passenger cancels)
  ACCEPTED   -> STARTED   (driver starts)
  ACCEPTED   -> CANCELLED (driver or passenger cancels)
  STARTED    -> COMPLETED (driver completes)

  Nothing else is allowed. COMPLETED and CANCELLED are final.

PART 11 - PROBLEMS AND SOLUTIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------

  "Cannot connect to the database"
     -> The database is not running. Run the STEP 1 command in Part 1.
        Check with: netstat -an | findstr 5544

  "Port 3000 already in use"
     -> The website is probably already running. Close the other window or
        change PORT=3000 to another number in the .env file.

  The map is grey / no streets visible
     -> The street pictures come from the internet (OpenStreetMap).
        Check the internet connection. Everything else works without it.

  "Email or password is incorrect"
     -> Check the exact email and password from Part 2 of this file.

  Nothing appears on the map after "Calculate Route"
     -> Make sure BOTH pins (A and B) are on the map before calculating.

  I want a fresh start with clean demo data
     -> In the project folder run:  npm run db:setup
